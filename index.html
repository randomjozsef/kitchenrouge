Pause or resume the run. In co-op, either player can pause the team. Use E / Space or the ability button for active abilities; Rook and Frost have passive-only kits. Bastion braces, Vex leaves a decoy, Fuse releases a blast, and Sage heals once per floor.

Never leave a courier behind.

P1 and P2 labels identify players; outfit colors identify characters. Both share rooms, coins and keys, but have separate recipes, upgrades and lives. The courier who collects a recipe gets its effects. Clear the combat room to revive a downed partner with 1 life. Both down? The run ends.

Major bosses: Boiler Heart (floor 3), Ash Warden (6), The Silent Deep (9), The Unravelled (12). Outlined danger zones show their countdown before turning solid and dealing damage. Clear the last boss and use the END SHIFT hatch to win. Completion marks save per courier. Maps use fixed 6×6 grids with randomized branching layouts. Standard rooms fit entirely on screen. Only larger rooms follow your courier. Damage numbers show projectile hits and lost lives; a brief red border signals damage to your courier. Keys are shared in co-op; collect a free key in the starting room from floor 2. Chests and item-room entrances each cost 1 key from floor 2; floor 1 is free. No free room-clear or next-floor healing. Cafes restore 1 life. Flawless bosses grant blessings; optional spicy deals cost only the collecting courier maximum lives. Boss completion marks stay saved. There are no permanent stat upgrades or token rewards.
